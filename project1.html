Corporate Bond Yields

Financial econometrics

Problem Statement

How do macroeconomic factors (SBI rate, exchange rate, inflation) and firm-specific factors (firm size, DER) affect corporate bond yields in Indonesia? Understanding this is crucial for investors to manage risk and for companies to determine optimal bond issuance pricing.

Methodology

The study utilized Multiple Linear Regression on a panel dataset. The data was collected from various corporate bonds listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX) over a 5-year period. Assumption tests (normality, multicollinearity, heteroscedasticity) were conducted to ensure the model's validity.

Results & Insight

  • SBI Rate: Berpengaruh positif signifikan. Kenaikan suku bunga acuan membuat yield obligasi ikut naik untuk mengompensasi risiko.
  • Exchange Rate: Berpengaruh positif signifikan. Depresiasi Rupiah meningkatkan kekhawatiran investor terhadap inflasi, sehingga menuntut yield yang lebih tinggi.
  • Firm Size: Berpengaruh negatif. Perusahaan besar dianggap lebih aman (low risk), sehingga yield yang ditawarkan lebih rendah.

Conclusion

Investors should closely monitor central bank policies and currency stability, as these macro variables are the main drivers of bond yield fluctuations. For corporations, maintaining a strong balance sheet (large firm size) is key to lowering borrowing costs.
